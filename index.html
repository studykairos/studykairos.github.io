Kairos support

Kairos records a time-lapse while you study and gives you a Focus Score. It works without an account, and then nothing leaves your phone.

Questions, bugs or a report about someone: kairos.support101@gmail.com. We answer reports within 24 hours.

Common questions

  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account. It removes everything you uploaded.
  • Stop uploading sessions: turn off "Count my sessions" in Settings → Account.
  • Report or block someone: press and hold their name on a group's leaderboard.
  • Delete a session: press and hold it in History. Sessions and videos live only on your phone.
  • Camera or Photos access: iOS Settings → Kairos. Kairos can only add to Photos, never read it.
